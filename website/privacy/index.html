Privacy Policy

NextSet for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. In short

NextSet does not collect any personal data. The app works entirely on your devices: there is no account, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to me.

3. Data stored on your devices

NextSet stores your timers (durations and optional names) and your settings (for example countdown, haptics, sounds and notifications) locally in the app’s storage on your iPhone and Apple Watch. This data never leaves your devices except for the sync described below, and I have no access to it. It is deleted when you delete the app.

4. Sync between iPhone and Apple Watch

Your timers are synchronized between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. The transfer is handled by Apple’s operating systems between your own devices; no server operated by me is involved.

5. Notifications and Live Activities

The notification at the end of a rest and the Live Activity on the Lock Screen are created locally on your device. No push service is used. You can allow or revoke notifications at any time in the iOS Settings app.

6. Siri and Shortcuts

If you start or end a rest with Siri, Apple processes your voice request according to Apple’s privacy policy. NextSet only receives the resulting action, for example “start a rest of 90 seconds”.

7. App Store, purchase and statistics

NextSet is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Downloads, purchases and payments are processed by Apple; Apple’s privacy policy applies (apple.com/legal/privacy). Apple provides me with aggregated statistics, such as the number of downloads, that do not identify you. Crash reports and usage statistics are only shared with me if you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device settings; this data is also provided by Apple without identifying you.

8. This website

This website is a static site. It does not use cookies, tracking or content from third parties. When you visit it, the hosting provider automatically processes technical access data in server log files (for example IP address, date and time of access, requested page and browser type). This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

If you contact me by email, I use your message and email address only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). To exercise your rights, please contact me at the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, is one competent authority: lda.bayern.de.

10. Children

NextSet does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

11. Changes

I will update this privacy policy if the app or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available on this page.
